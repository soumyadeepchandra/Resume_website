Research

AI for hardware3D IC thermal modelingPhysics informed learningNeural operatorsVideo understanding

Therm3D. I am developing a geometry aware neural operator for near real time steady state and transient thermal prediction in 3D heterogeneous integrated circuits. The work combines automated COMSOL data generation, coordinate conditioned prediction, and physical constraints such as energy conservation and boundary conditions. On unseen transient power maps, it achieved 1.09°C RMSE with approximately 10,000–20,000× faster inference than FEM.

ThermAl. My earlier work uses a physics informed U-Net to predict on-chip temperature from power maps. It reached approximately 200× faster inference than finite element simulation, with 0.71°C RMSE in the reported experiments.
